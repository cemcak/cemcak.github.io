# SartorX Support

SartorX converts a photo to the format, pixel size and file size an upload form requires. It runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

## Contact

Write to [support.filemeup@gmail.com](mailto:support.filemeup@gmail.com).

It helps if you include:

- your iPhone model and iOS version,
- the rule you were trying to meet, for example "JPG, max 300 KB, 600 x 600 px",
- what you expected and what happened instead.

You don't need to send the photo. If a screenshot explains the problem better, check that it shows nothing you'd rather keep private.

## Common questions

### How do I start?

Type or paste the requirement from the form on the first screen, scan it with the camera, or pick a preset. Then choose up to 20 photos and tap Convert.

### The app says a photo doesn't fit. What now?

Some rules can't all be met at once, for example a small file size at a large pixel size. The card under the photo suggests a way out and shows what it costs. Nothing that lowers quality or changes the pixel size is applied until you tap it.

### Where do my converted photos go?

Nowhere until you choose. On the results screen, Save adds them to your Photos library and Share hands them to the app you pick. SartorX keeps no copy of its own.

### Saving to Photos doesn't work.

SartorX needs permission to add photos. Open the Settings app, find SartorX, and allow it to add to Photos.

### Scan can't use the camera.

Open the Settings app, find SartorX, and allow Camera. You can also scan without the camera: pick a screenshot, an image file or a PDF of the requirement instead.

### I can't find SartorX in the share sheet.

In Photos, select up to 20 photos and tap Share. If SartorX isn't in the row of apps, look under More at the end of the row and add it there. The share sheet takes photos only.

### The scan or the pasted text was read wrongly.

Check the rules shown before you continue. If one is wrong, set the format, size and dimensions by hand on the target screen. Sending us the requirement text helps us improve this.

### Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone and the app makes no network connection. See the [Privacy Policy](https://cemcak.github.io).

### How do I delete my data?

The app stores only your settings, your own presets and the History list, on your iPhone. You can clear History in the app. Deleting the app removes everything.
